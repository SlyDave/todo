# Short imperative title

## Context

What forced a decision, and the constraints that were real at the time. Someone
reading this in a year should understand the situation without asking anyone.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly in the active voice.

## Alternatives considered

Each option genuinely on the table, and the specific reason it was rejected.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it forecloses. Include the
bad consequences; they are the reason the record exists.
